## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is PRSA free?**  
A: Yes, PRSA is completely free to download and use. The link above provides the full version with no paywalls or subscriptions.

**Q: Will PRSA work on my old PC?**  
A: Yes! PRSA includes multiple quality presets. The "Potato" preset is optimized for old hardware and maintains playable FPS on integrated graphics.

**Q: Is PRSA compatible with SAMP or MTA?**  
A: PRSA works with SAMP and MTA, but some advanced visual effects may be limited in multiplayer. Most texture enhancements work perfectly.

**Q: Can I use PRSA with other GTA SA mods?**  
A: Yes, PRSA is compatible with most CLEO mods, ASI plugins, vehicle mods, and weapon mods. Avoid other graphics mods (ENB, other texture packs) as they conflict.

**Q: Do I need to downgrade my Steam version?**  
A: Yes, Steam and Rockstar Launcher versions must be downgraded to v1.0 US. The PRSA installer includes an automatic downgrader, or you can use a manual downgrade tool.

**Q: What FPS can I expect with PRSA?**  
A: Performance varies by preset: Ultra (RTX 2060): 45-60 FPS, High (GTX 1060): 50-70 FPS, Medium (GTX 750 Ti): 60-80 FPS, Low (integrated): 30-50 FPS.

**Q: How do I uninstall PRSA?**  
A: Run the installer again and select "Uninstall" or restore your backup folder. The installer automatically creates a backup before installation.

**Q: Why is my game crashing?**  
A: Make sure you have the downgraded v1.0 US executable. Also ensure your graphics drivers are up to date.

**Q: Does PRSA support widescreen and high resolutions?**  
A: Yes, PRSA includes widescreen fixes and supports all modern resolutions up to 4K and ultrawide (21:9, 32:9).

**Q: Can I use PRSA with GTA Underground or other total conversions?**  
A: PRSA may conflict with mods that add new map areas. Test compatibility; you may need to install PRSA before the total conversion.

**Q: How do I change graphics settings after installation?**  
A: Launch GTA SA, go to Options → Display Settings. Some advanced effects can be toggled in the `prsa.ini` configuration file.

**Q: Is there a performance guide?**  
A: Yes, check the `PRSA_Readme.txt` file in your GTA SA folder for detailed performance optimization tips.
